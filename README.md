## Irakurle Overview

It treats strategy in a reinforcement learning exercise with explicit rules, scoring mechanisms, and winning conditions. It allows junior consultants to measure progress, iterate improvements that compound organizational knowledge over time.

## Strategy Case Ontology

There are 14 distinct strategy consulting cases that a business strategist needs to excel as a master. These cases are organized in a graph. 

### Graph Engineering

A graph is the simplest data structure you already understand from real life: things, and the relationships between them.
- Nodes are the things: a person, a document, a company, a function in your codebase.
- Edges are the relationships: Alice works_at Acme, parse_config() calls read_file(), Invoice-4521 issued_by Acme.
- Properties are details attached to either: Alice has role: engineer, the works_at edge has since: 2023.
